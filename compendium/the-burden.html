Moment 1 of 7

The noise of the people

Babylon’s flood story begins with number: twelve hundred years had not gone by, the land spread wide, the people grew many, and their noise kept the god awake. Enlil sent plague, then famine, then the flood, and before the end his tears flowed down. Japan’s heaven looks down on a land loud with gods like buzzing flies, and Izanami swears to strangle a thousand of the living every day.

The earth filled with violence

Genesis gives a cause the others do not: the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and the earth was filled with violence. The Buddhists date it to the first hoard and the first theft.
