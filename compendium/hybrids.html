Moment 1 of 11

The sons of God saw the daughters of men

Genesis says the sons of God saw that the daughters of men were fair and took them wives, and says no more about who the sons of God were. The books of Enoch say two hundred came down on Hermon in the days of Jared. The Syriac chronicles make them the children of Seth come down from their holy mountain, and the Latvian Bible gives them the folk songs’ own name, Dieva dēli, the riders who court the Sun’s daughter.
