“I have not yet decreed”

Text: Erra and Išum, Akkadian, five tablets; the author names himself as Kabti-ilāni-Marduk of the Dābibī family; proposed dates from the eleventh to the eighth century BCE. Edition and translation: Enrique Jiménez, electronic Babylonian Library (eBL), 2023.
