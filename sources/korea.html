古記云。昔有桓因(謂帝釋也)庶子桓雄。數意天下。貪求人世。父知子意。下視三危太伯可以弘益人間。乃授天符印三箇。遣往理之。雄率徒三千。降於太伯山頂(即太伯今妙香山)神壇樹下。謂之神市。是謂桓雄天王也。將風伯雨師雲師。而主穀主命主病主刑主善惡。凡主人間三百六十餘事。在世理化。 "The Old Record says: Of old there was Hwanin (that is, Śakra) and his son by a secondary wife, Hwanung, who again and again set his mind on the world below and coveted the human world. The father knew his son's mind; looking down on the Three Dangers and Taebaek, he saw they could be of broad benefit to mankind. So he gave him three heavenly seals and sent him to go and govern it. Ung led a company of three thousand and came down to the top of Mount Taebaek (that is, Taebaek, now Myohyang-san), under the tree of the divine altar; they called it the City of God; this is Hwanung the Heavenly King. He took with him the Wind Master, the Rain Master and the Cloud Master, and had charge of grain, of life, of sickness, of punishment, of good and evil: in all, of some three hundred and sixty human matters. He was in the world and brought it to order by teaching." (house)

時有一熊一虎。同穴而居。常祈于神雄。願化為人。時神遺靈艾一炷。蒜二十枚曰。爾輩食之。不見日光百日。便得人形。熊虎得而食之。忌三七日。熊得女身。虎不能忌。而不得人身。熊女者無與為婚。故每於壇樹下呪願有孕。雄乃假化而婚之。孕生子。號曰壇君王儉。……御國一千五百年。……後還隱於阿斯達為山神。壽一千九百八歲。 "At that time a bear and a tiger lived in one cave and prayed constantly to the divine Ung, wishing to become human. The god gave them a sprig of sacred mugwort and twenty cloves of garlic and said: "Eat these, and do not see the sunlight for a hundred days, and you will get human form." The bear and the tiger took them and ate; they kept the taboo twenty-one days; the bear got a woman's body; the tiger could not keep it and got no human body. The bear-woman had no one to marry, so under the altar tree she prayed each time to conceive. Ung then took on a borrowed form and married her; she conceived and bore a son, called Dangun Wanggeom. … He ruled the country fifteen hundred years. … Later he went back and hid at Asadal and became a mountain god, at the age of one thousand nine hundred and eight." (house) "His son, Whan-ung, being affected by celestial ennui, obtained permission to descend to earth and found a mundane kingdom. Armed with this warrant, Whan-ung with three thousand spirit companions descended upon Ta-băk Mountain, now known as Myo-hyang San, in the province of P'yŭng-an, Korea. … He governed through his three vice-regents, the "Wind General," the "Rain Governor," and the "Cloud Teacher," but as he had not yet taken human shape, he found it difficult to assume control of a purely human kingdom."
