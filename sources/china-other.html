往古之時，四極廢，九州裂，天不兼覆，地不周載，火爁炎而不滅，水浩洋而不息，猛獸食顓民，鷙鳥攫老弱，於是女媧煉五色石以補蒼天，斷鼇足以立四極。殺黑龍以濟冀州，積蘆灰以止淫水。蒼天補，四極正，淫水涸，冀州平，狡蟲死，顓民生。 "In the far past the four poles gave way and the nine provinces split; heaven did not wholly cover, earth did not wholly carry; fire blazed and would not go out, water spread wide and would not stop; fierce beasts ate the good people, birds of prey snatched the old and the weak. Then Nüwa smelted five-coloured stones to patch the blue sky and cut off the legs of the great turtle to set up the four poles. She killed the black dragon to save Jizhou and heaped reed-ash to stop the wild waters. The blue sky was patched, the four poles stood straight, the wild waters dried, Jizhou was level, the cunning creatures died, the good people lived." (house) 女媧，陰帝，佐虙戲治者也。三皇時天不足西北，故補之 "Nüwa, the Yin Sovereign, who helped Fuxi govern. In the time of the Three August Ones heaven was deficient in the north-west, so she patched it" (house)
