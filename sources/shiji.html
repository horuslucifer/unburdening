昔在顓頊，命南正重以司天，北正黎以司地。唐虞之際，紹重黎之後，使復典之，至於夏商，故重黎氏世序天地。其在周，程伯休甫其後也。當周宣王時，失其守而為司馬氏。司馬氏世典周史。 "Of old under Zhuanxu, Chong, Regulator of the South, was commanded to manage heaven and Li, Regulator of the North, to manage earth. In the days of Yao and Shun the line of Chong and Li was continued and made to hold the office again, down through Xia and Shang; so the house of Chong and Li ordered heaven and earth generation after generation. Under Zhou, Earl Xiufu of Cheng was their heir. In the time of King Xuan they lost the charge and became the Sima clan. The Sima clan kept the records of Zhou generation after generation." (house) "ce Fou P'ou, comte de Tch'eng, passait pour être issu d'un certain Li qui, sous le très antique empereur Tchoan-hiu, avait été nommé fonctionnaire préposé à la terre; or, d'autre part, Li avait pour collègue Tchong qui était chargé de s'occuper du ciel et qui peut être regardé comme le premier des grands astrologues."

察日行以處位太白。曰西方，秋……日庚、辛，主殺。殺失者，罰出太白。太白失行，以其捨命國。其出行十八舍二百四十日而入。入東方，伏行十一舍百三十日；其入西方，伏行三舍十六日而出。當出不出，當入不入，是謂失舍，不有破軍，必有國君之篡。 "Watch the sun's course to place Taibai. It is the west, autumn … the days geng and xin; it governs killing. When killing goes wrong, the punishment comes out of Taibai. When Taibai loses its course, the lodge it occupies dooms a state. It comes out and travels eighteen lodges, 240 days, then goes in. Going in in the east, it travels hidden eleven lodges, 130 days; going in in the west, it travels hidden three lodges, sixteen days, then comes out. When it should come out and does not, should go in and does not, that is called losing its lodge: if there is no broken army there is a usurpation of the ruler." (house) 太白，大臣也，其號上公。其他名殷星、太正、營星、觀星、宮星、明星、大衰、大澤、終星、大相、天浩、序星、月緯。大司馬位謹候此。 "Taibai is the great minister; its title is Supreme Duke. Its other names: Yin Star, Great Regulator, Camp Star, Watching Star, Palace Star, Bright Star, Great Decline, Great Marsh, Final Star, Great Minister, Heaven's Vastness, Order Star, Moon's Woof. The Grand Marshal's office watches it with care." (house) 用兵象太白……太白伏也，以出兵，兵有殃。 "In using troops take Taibai as the model … When Taibai is hidden, to send out troops brings disaster on them." (house)

'These three spirit mountains are said to lie in the Bohai, not far from men; but when one is about to arrive, a wind draws the boat away. Some, it is said, once reached them: the immortals and the drug of deathlessness are all there; their creatures, birds and beasts, are all white, and their palace gates are of gold and silver. Before one arrives they look like clouds; on arrival the three spirit mountains lie, on the contrary, beneath the water. As one draws near, the wind always pulls one away; in the end no one can reach them. Every ruler of the age longed for them.' 'The Yellow Emperor mounted; ministers and palace women who went up with him were more than seventy; then the dragon rose. The lesser officials who could not get on all clung to the dragon's whiskers; the whiskers came out, they fell, and the Yellow Emperor's bow fell. The people, gazing up after the Yellow Emperor had gone up to heaven, clasped his bow and the whiskers and wailed.' 「嗟乎！吾誠得如黃帝，吾視去妻子如脫屣耳。」 'Ah! If I could truly be like the Yellow Emperor, I would look on leaving wife and children as no more than kicking off a sandal.' (Emperor Wu of Han)
