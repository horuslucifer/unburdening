Jerome: "Pro eo quod nos interpretati sumus ob facilitatem intelligentiae, Quomodo cecidisti de coelo, lucifer, qui mane oriebaris, in Hebraico, ut verbum exprimamus ad verbum, legitur, Quomodo cecidisti de coelo, ulula fili diluculi. Significatur autem aliis verbis lucifer; et dicitur ei quod flere debeat et lugere, qui quondam sic [erat] gloriosus, ut fulgori luciferi comparatus sit." (house) "Where we, for ease of understanding, translated "How you fell from heaven, lucifer, who rose at morning," the Hebrew, to render it word for word, reads: "How you fell from heaven: howl, son of the dawn." Lucifer is meant in other words; and he is told that he must weep and mourn, he who was once so glorious that he was compared to the brightness of the morning star.
