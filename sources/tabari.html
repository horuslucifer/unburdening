And see ye not Lat and Ozza, / And Manat the third besides? / ... the devil suggested an expression of the thoughts which for many a day had possessed his soul; and put into his mouth words of reconciliation and compromise ... / These are the exalted Females, / And verily their Intercession is to be hoped for. / ... In the evening Gabriel visited him; and the Prophet recited the Sura unto him. And Gabriel said, What is this that thou hast done? thou hast repeated before the people words that I never gave unto thee. / ... So the two Satanic verses were in the mouth of every one of the unbelievers ..." Ṭabarī: "ألقى عليه الشيطان كلمتين: تلك الغرانقة العلى، وإن شفاعتهن لترجى" "قال: ما جئتك بهاتين، فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلم: افتريت على الله، وقلت على الله ما لم يقل" "وكان ذلك الحرفان اللذان ألقى الشيطان على لسان رسوله قد وقعا في فم كل مشرك" (house) "Satan cast upon him two sayings: "Those are the high cranes, and their intercession is surely hoped for." ... [Gabriel] said, "I did not bring you these two." And the Messenger of God said, "I have forged against God, and said of God what He did not say." ... And those two sayings which Satan had cast upon the tongue of His messenger had fallen into the mouth of every idolater.
