“It is clear therefore from their dignities and their deaths that the …”

19. The arts kept. "Though some say that the Tuatha Dé Danann were demons, seeing that they came unperceived ... howbeit they learnt knowledge and poetry; for every obscurity of art and every clearness of reading, and every subtlety of crafts, for that reason, derive their origin from the Tuatha Dé Danann. And though the Faith came, those arts were not put away, for they are good, and no demon ever did good." Conc … still in copyright, so only a fragment
